Account & Data Deletion — PSAS

Pegasus Sports & Psychology Performance · Last updated: 19/08/2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your PSAS account and the personal data associated with it. PSAS is the fitness class booking app operated by Pegasus Sports & Psychology Performance.

How to request deletion

PSAS accounts are deleted by our staff on request — there is currently no in-app self-service delete option. To request deletion:

  1. Email us at admin.psas@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account (the one you used to sign in with Google).
  2. Include the subject line "Account deletion request" and confirm you'd like your PSAS account and associated data deleted.
  3. We will verify the request and confirm by email once your account has been deleted.

Request account deletion

What gets deleted

Once your deletion request is processed, we remove:

  • Your account profile — name, email, profile photo, phone number
  • Your membership and credit balance records
  • Your booking, appointment, and waiting-list history

What we retain, and for how long

Some records are kept even after account deletion where we have a legal or accounting obligation to do so — specifically payment and invoice records (plan purchased, amount, currency, transaction date), which we retain for up to 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping requirements. This data is de-linked from your profile information and is not used for any purpose other than accounting and legal compliance.

Partial deletion

If you'd only like specific information corrected or removed (for example, an outdated phone number) rather than deleting your whole account, mention that in your email instead and we'll action it without closing your account.

Questions

See our Privacy Policy for more detail on what we collect and why, or contact us at admin.psas@gmail.com with any questions.
