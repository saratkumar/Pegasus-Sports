Common Questions

  • How do I book a class? — Sign in, go to the Timetable tab, choose Classes, Appointments, or Facilities, pick a date, and tap Book Now.
  • How do I buy or renew a membership? — Go to the Membership tab and choose a Trial, Drop-In, Credits, or Monthly plan.
  • How do I cancel a booking? — Go to the Bookings tab, find the booking, and cancel it from there.
  • How do I update my profile or mobile number? — Tap your profile icon at the top right, update your details, and tap Save Changes.
  • How do I delete my account? — See our Account & Data Deletion page.
